The Author

Tvisha

Tvisha is an electronics engineer by profession, working in California in technology. She came to writing late. She told stories long before she had books to read—there was no library where she grew up, so she read whatever paper reached the house and invented the rest, usually out loud.

India-born and Gujarati by heritage, she has spent her adult life keeping the culture alive in her home and neighbourhood, through food, classical dance and the artwork of Gujarat.

Tapovan comes from that same instinct. It is an act of preservation as much as a novel.

She lives in California with her husband and son.
