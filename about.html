The Author

Tvisha

Tvisha is an electronics engineer by profession, working in California in technology. She came to writing late, though she told stories long before she had books to read.

India-born and Gujarati by heritage, she has spent her adult life keeping culture alive through food, classical dance and the artwork of Gujarat. Distance, for her, made preservation feel urgent.

Tapovan comes from that same instinct. It is an act of preservation as much as a novel.

She lives in California with her husband and son.
