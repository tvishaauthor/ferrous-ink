The Author

Tvisha

Stories begin long before they are written.

Tvisha is an engineer by profession and a writer by instinct. She told stories long before she had books to read—there was no library where she grew up, so she read whatever paper reached the house and invented the rest, usually out loud. Studies and work took the next few decades. The stories waited.

Indian-born and Gujarati by heritage, she has spent much of her adult life preserving the culture she grew up with—through food, classical dance, art, language, and story.

Tapovan comes from that same instinct. It is an act of preservation as much as a novel.
